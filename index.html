Classroom: laptop to panel, no internet

The free BeamCast Desktop app for Windows connects the laptop straight to a Promethean panel, smart TV or projector. It doesn't need the school Wi-Fi or the internet.

BeamCast Desktop for Windows

Windows 10 and 11. One file, nothing to install. Download it at home or anywhere with internet, then copy it to the laptop.

How to use it in class

  1. On the Promethean panel, open the Screen Share app.
  2. On the laptop, open BeamCastDesktop.exe and click Find panel.
  3. Click the panel's name in the list. The laptop screen appears on the panel.
  4. To stop, click Find panel again and choose Disconnect.

First time only: if Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for new apps that aren't sold in a store.

Online: any device, anywhere

With internet, BeamCast works in the browser on computers, phones, tablets and TVs. Nothing to install.

  1. On the device you want to show, open BeamCast and choose Share this screen. A code and QR code appear.
  2. On the screen that will watch, scan the QR code, or open BeamCast, choose Watch on this device and type the code.
  3. Check that both screens show the same 2-digit number, approve, and start sharing.

Open BeamCast online

Tip: in Chrome or Edge, use “Install app” in the address bar or menu to add BeamCast to your device. It then opens even when you're offline.

Safe by design

  • Nothing about you is collected

    No accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. This website loads nothing from other companies.

  • Only who you allow

    In class, you choose the panel yourself. Online, every viewer must be approved after checking a matching number. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

  • Encrypted

    Wireless display uses Windows' built-in encrypted link. Online casting is encrypted end to end and goes straight between the two devices.

  • Check your download

    Compare the SHA-256 checksum above with the file you downloaded. In PowerShell, run: Get-FileHash BeamCastDesktop.exe

Help

The panel doesn't appear in the list

Make sure the Screen Share app is open on the panel and allows Windows / Miracast devices. Switch Wi-Fi on in Windows (it doesn't need to be connected to a network) and stay within a few metres of the panel.

The app says the laptop can't do wireless display

Use the second option in the app: if the laptop and panel share a Wi-Fi, open the panel's browser and type the address and code the app shows. Or connect an HDMI cable.

The picture lags

Wireless display is the smoothest option. In the Wi-Fi option, choose Picture: Smooth, and move closer to the router.

Is this website always available?

Yes. It is a simple static site on GitHub Pages with secure HTTPS and no server to break. After your first visit it also opens without internet. The classroom app never needs this website once downloaded.
